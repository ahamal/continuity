Better representation of states

This is where the work stands. Stages 3 to 5 run, and they show what the current representation cannot express. Nothing on this page is implemented yet.

What stages 3 to 5 leave open

  • A state is only raw coordinates. The agent learns "at (4, 4), right does nothing". It cannot learn "there is a wall at x = 5" or "the edge is at x = 9 for every y".
  • Only two levels of generality are stored. One is the action alone, the other is the whole state with the action. The levels between them, such as the action with x alone, are never written.
  • Storing every level does not scale. A situation with n facts has 2n combinations of them.
  • The agent does not know the extent of its world. It plans through cells outside the grid, which is why its search runs out in the walls scenario.
  • There is one thing in the world. The agent is the only identity, and it is given. Board games have many things of several kinds, and none of them is the agent.
  • Stored spread goes unused. An entry keeps every outcome it has seen with a count, but predictions use only the most frequent one.

Questions for this stage

  1. What should a state be made of, so that one representation serves the line, the grid and board games?
  2. How do the levels of generality between "always" and "exactly here" come to exist without storing every combination?
  3. How do things other than the agent enter the state, and how are they grouped into kinds?
  4. How much of what the world provides today should stay built in?
